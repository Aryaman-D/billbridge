The process

From “what if”
to what’s next.

You don’t need to be a policy expert to have an idea worth sharing. We’re here to make the process feel a little more possible.

01 / LEARN

Start with the basics.

Understand the building blocks of a bill and how an idea becomes a proposal your state legislature can consider.

02 / SHARE

Tell us your idea.

Describe the problem, who it affects, and what you think should change. Our planned review fee is $5 per idea.

03 / DEVELOP

Build toward action.

If selected, we’ll work with you to refine your proposal and identify potential legislative supporters. Introduction or passage is never guaranteed.
